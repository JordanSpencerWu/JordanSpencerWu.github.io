The inherent problem with this concept is that we assume that if a person is not in a positive mood (or whatever we think a positive person should look or act like), then they are somehow wrong, bad, or inadequate. A growth mindset is a belief that people, including oneself, can change their talents, abilities, and intelligence.

a growth mindset are more mentally primed to approach and take on challenges, take advantage of feedback, adopt the most effective problem-solving strategies, provide developmental feedback to their employees, and be effortful and persistent in seeking to accomplish goals.

A learning mindset involves being motivated toward increasing one’s competence and mastering something new.

They are also persistent, adaptable, willing to cooperate, and tend to perform at a higher level.

People with a deliberative mindset have a heightened receptiveness to all kinds of information as a way to ensure that they think and act as optimally as possible.

People with an implemental mindset, as the name suggests, are more focused on implementing decisions, which closes them off to new and different ideas and information.

People with a promotion mindset are focused on winning and gains. They identify a specific purpose, goal, or destination and prioritize making progress toward it.

People with a prevention mindset, however, are focused on avoiding losses and preventing problems at all costs.

those with a promotion mindset are more prone to positive thinking, more open to change, more likely to persist despite challenges and setbacks, and demonstrate higher levels of task performance and innovative behavior compared to people with a prevention mindset.
