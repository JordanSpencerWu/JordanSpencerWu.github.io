The performance of a Text typeface is best judged by viewing it — and using it — at its intended size in a passage of text.

the details of the typefaces themselves really do matter.

When we enlarge a word or phrase that contains a typeface’s most distinctive glyphs, we unearth all sorts of information about what makes that typeface tick.

We discover how the space inside and between letters is as important as the strokes of the letters themselves, and how the shape of one letter affects the shape of the others.

Once this knowledge is acquired, it becomes a valuable and instantly accessible piece of a type user’s skill set

Each of these characteristics can contribute to the overall appearance of a typeface — how it changes the look of a word, a paragraph, or a page. And, just as importantly, they can be functional characteristics, telling us what a typeface is capable of.
