Cognac & Armagnac — French brandies made from grapes. Deeply tied to luxury culture. Hennessy, Rémy Martin, Courvoisier at the entry level. Louis XIII, Armagnac Delord at the top.
• Tequila & Mezcal — Made from agave plant. Mezcal especially has become a serious connoisseur category. Brands like Don Julio 1942, Clase Azul, and Fortaleza are respected.
• Calvados — French apple brandy. Very old money European. Largely undiscovered by mainstream culture which makes it interesting.
• Rum — Sugarcane based. Premium aged rums from Barbados and Jamaica like Foursquare and Appleton 21 are genuinely world class.
• Pisco — South American grape spirit. Emerging luxury category.

Aged Rum is the underrated alternative:
• Foursquare from Barbados is considered by serious enthusiasts as world class
• Appleton Estate 21 from Jamaica is smooth and deeply aged
• Much lower price point than Cognac for equivalent quality — good value knowledge to have
